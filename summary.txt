World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

Abu Dhabi talks ended with no ceasefire but a 1,000-1,500-prisoner swap, the war's biggest release (AP). Russia fired 163 drones overnight; six substations hit, 135,000 lost power in Odesa (ISW). Drones set fire to Russia's Ust-Luga oil port, injuring three (Reuters). NATO scrambled jets after four drones entered Baltic airspace (Reuters). Iran said Hormuz, shut since February, stays closed until the U.S. meets seven conditions; its navy said three tankers caught fire after hitting mines, unconfirmed (Al Mayadeen). Rubio said sanctions and a naval blockade mean 'no need for major combat operations' for now (Reuters). Trump called the Iran war militarily over and said he expects to meet Xi and Putin at APEC (Reuters). The DOJ charged three Iran-backed operatives over nearly 20 attacks, including a plot against RAF Fairford (Times of Israel). A 41-year-old U.S. Army officer was arrested at NATO's Imjin Barracks in Gloucester on suspicion of spying for Russia (BBC). Israeli forces killed at least seven in Gaza, a father and 13-year-old son among them (Reuters). Israel's Knesset was sworn in, Netanyahu's bloc at 53 of 120 and Eisenkot's at 48 (Reuters). At COP31, five nations pledged rooftop solar for 300 million homes by 2030 (AP); Guterres warned of a 'path to destruction' (UN). China's trade surplus hit $1.04 trillion in ten months, $105.3 billion in October (Reuters). U.S. prices rose 0.4% in October, core 0.3%; traders priced a December Fed hike as all but certain as the 10-year yield topped 5% (BLS; CNBC). Democrat Scott Colom won Mississippi's Senate seat, its first Black senator since Reconstruction (AP). The House voted 211-204 to send Hegseth's impeachment to committee, killing it (Reuters). DR Congo confirmed 11,042 Ebola cases and 5,387 deaths, the second-largest outbreak ever (Reuters). Amnesty said UN peacekeepers failed to stop the March massacre at Abiemnom, South Sudan, killing 213 (AP).
