Wednesday, 11 November 2026

Russia and Ukraine agreed in principle to the largest prisoner exchange of the war as two days of U.S.-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi ended without a breakthrough, AP and Reuters reported. Envoy Jared Kushner said about 1,000 prisoners would be freed by each side, numbers due 12 November; he and Steve Witkoff travel to Moscow to meet Vladimir Putin, then Dublin to see Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said in Paris that sanctions and a naval blockade are squeezing Iran so hard there is 'no need for major combat operations' for now, Reuters reported, while warning the U.S. military stands ready if Iran crosses red lines. Tehran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei denied any talks were under way, and the U.S. Justice Department charged three Iran-backed operatives over alleged plots in the United States and Europe.

World leaders met in Antalya for COP31, where UN chief Antonio Guterres warned the world is on a 'path to destruction'; the U.S. stayed away for a second year, Reuters reported. Israel's new parliament was sworn in with no government after a deadlocked election, Reuters reported: Benjamin Netanyahu's bloc holds 53 of 120 seats, Gadi Eisenkot's 48. Israeli strikes killed at least seven people in Gaza, local health officials said, as rain flooded tent camps sheltering displaced families, the UN said.

Pope Leo XIV returned to Peru, his adopted homeland, to a hero's welcome in Lima, where the Vatican said three million people turned out, CNN reported. An Ebola patient flown from Nigeria to Geneva was released from hospital as confirmed cases in Africa's outbreak passed 11,000, the second-worst on record, Reuters reported. In Washington, House Republicans blocked an attempt to impeach Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, and Democrat Scott Colom won Mississippi's Senate seat, AP reported. British police arrested a 41-year-old U.S. Army officer on suspicion of spying for Russia, CNN reported.
